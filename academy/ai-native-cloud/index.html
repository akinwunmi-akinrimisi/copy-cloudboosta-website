Who this programme is for

A year in cloud. Ready to add AI to it.

This is for people already working in cloud who want to understand, build and run AI-enabled solutions before it becomes a required skill. It is not suitable for complete beginners.

You will fit if you are
  • A cloud engineer with a year or more of hands-on AWS, Azure or GCP
  • A cloud support, operations or solutions engineer being asked to bring AI into the environment you run
  • A cloud engineer who wants the AWS Certified AI Practitioner credential backed by real builds, not flashcards
  • A cloud engineer looking to accelerate into a senior or lead role
Prerequisites
  • At least one year of hands-on cloud computing experience, on AWS, Azure or GCP
  • Comfortable in Linux and on the command line
  • Networking fundamentals
  • Basic scripting or software development

Everything hands-on runs through the AWS Console, the CLI and managed services, taught as they are needed. New to cloud? Start with the Academy pathways and come back for this one.

Curriculum

Understand. Build. Connect. Secure. Operate. Defend.

One Saturday a week. Each one is two live lectures and two live two-hour labs, with two hours of recorded exam-depth material and an assignment lab to work through in your own time. Every week leaves you with something you built and a deliverable you can show at work.

Session 1Saturday 7 November 2026Week 1 · Understand and apply

What AI is, where it fits in cloud, and how it improves your cloud work today

A common understanding of modern AI, how AI workloads fit a cloud environment, and then straight into using AI as a productivity tool in real cloud work. The capstone brief lands this week.
  • AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI: what they are and how they differ
  • Foundation models and LLMs: tokens, context windows, inference, embeddings and vector search
  • The AWS AI service landscape, AI cloud architecture patterns and what drives AI workload cost
  • AI-assisted cloud engineering: Amazon Q Developer across the Console, the CLI and CloudFormation
  • Log and configuration analysis, AI-assisted incident response, spotting hallucinations and validating AI output
Live lab 1

Set up Bedrock model access, call it from code, read tokens and inference parameters, and build a cost estimate.

Live lab 2

An AI-assisted Console, CLI and CloudFormation task with Amazon Q Developer, plus troubleshooting a CloudWatch log sample.

Assignment lab

An end-to-end Bedrock chatbot: model selection, API integration and a working generative AI app.

Deliverable · an analysis of how AI could improve one of your cloud workflows, plus your capstone proposal
Session 2Saturday 14 November 2026Week 2 · Build

Building AI-powered cloud applications

From using AI tools to building applications that talk to foundation models directly, and choosing the right model for the job on cost and performance.
  • Foundation model APIs and Amazon Bedrock: SDKs, the Converse API, system prompts, structured responses, error handling
  • Model selection and inference parameters, and how each choice moves cost and performance
  • Customisation options and their trade-offs: in-context learning, RAG, fine-tuning, pre-training
  • How foundation models are evaluated: human evaluation, benchmarks, ROUGE, BLEU, BERTScore
  • Token usage, model pricing, request efficiency and caching
Live lab 1

Call Bedrock from code, configure system prompts and inference parameters.

Live lab 2

Compare models and settings side by side, build and demonstrate a working application, document why you chose the model.

Assignment lab

A multi-turn chatbot with system prompts, conversation history and Bedrock Guardrails, end to end.

Deliverable · a working AI application with its architecture and documented model-selection reasoning
Session 3Saturday 21 November 2026Week 3 · Connect

Knowledge-enabled AI with RAG, and n8n automation

A model does not know your organisation's documents. This week connects it to them natively with Bedrock Knowledge Bases, then introduces n8n as the no-code way to connect AI to data and trigger AWS workflows.
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation: why it exists, retrieval, context augmentation and grounded responses
  • Embeddings and semantic search, document ingestion and chunking strategies
  • Vector stores on AWS: Amazon OpenSearch, and when Aurora, RDS PostgreSQL, Neptune or DocumentDB fit
  • RAG evaluation, retrieval and storage costs
  • n8n fundamentals: triggers, nodes and workflows, self-hosted or cloud, connected to Bedrock by API key and webhook
Live lab 1

Build a RAG assistant over a sample document set with a Bedrock Knowledge Base backed by OpenSearch.

Live lab 2

Build an n8n workflow that calls Amazon Bedrock from an n8n node over a webhook.

Assignment lab

A full RAG pipeline built inside n8n with a vector database: both halves of the week in one project.

Deliverable · a working RAG application and a working n8n workflow connected to AWS
Session 4Saturday 28 November 2026Week 4 · Automate and secure

AI agents with controlled actions, secured in the same week they are introduced

From systems that retrieve information to systems that act. Because giving AI real capability without governance is the core engineering risk, you secure that access the same day you grant it.
  • AI agents, agent architecture, tool and function calling, MCP concepts
  • Building a native agent on Amazon Bedrock Agents and AgentCore: tool permissions, human approval, execution controls, failure modes
  • AI security: prompt injection, jailbreaking, data exposure, excessive agency, the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications
  • Least-privilege IAM, AWS Secrets Manager, Bedrock Guardrails, input and output validation
  • Responsible AI, bias and fairness, SageMaker Clarify, and the AWS governance and compliance toolset
Live lab 1

Build a native Bedrock agent on AgentCore with at least one controlled tool action.

Live lab 2

Build a least-privilege IAM role, move a secret into Secrets Manager, configure a Bedrock Guardrail for the agent.

Assignment lab

Take a prototype agent to production: AgentCore Runtime, Gateway-wired tools, Memory, identity-based access and observability.

Deliverable · a native Bedrock agent that performs a controlled action, with a documented security model and a responsible-AI review
Session 5Saturday 5 December 2026Week 5 · Operate and architect

Deploy, monitor, optimise and architect AI systems for production

Building the application is one part of the lifecycle. This week you deploy, observe, measure and optimise an AI workload through the Console and managed services, then bring it all together into a production-oriented architecture.
  • Deployment through the AWS Console and managed services, with an introduction to CloudFormation
  • AI FinOps: budgets, usage monitoring, cost optimisation, total cost of ownership
  • Logging, monitoring and tracing for AI workloads, and evaluating response quality, latency and token usage
  • Architecture trade-offs, scalability, reliability, performance, reference architectures and decision records
  • Multi-cloud awareness: Azure OpenAI and Google Vertex AI equivalents, and where ECS and EKS fit
Live lab 1

Deploy your capstone application through the Console and managed services, and instrument its cost metrics.

Live lab 2

Draw the capstone architecture, run a cost and TCO analysis, finalise security, deployment, observability and the decision record.

Assignment lab

A complete Bedrock and Lambda deployment, extended with cost monitoring and an architecture review.

Deliverable · a deployed AI application with observability, usage metrics and cost notes, and your final capstone ready to defend
Session 6 · CapstoneSaturday 12 December 2026Week 6 · Defend

Capstone presentation and technical defence

The final Saturday is session-based rather than lecture and lab: presentation preparation and the defence framework, then every participant presents, demonstrates and defends their capstone, followed by feedback and the programme wrap-up.
  • The problem, the proposed AI solution and its architecture
  • Model and service selection, knowledge integration, agent and tool integration
  • Security, responsible-AI and governance controls, deployment approach and observability
  • The cost model, the optimisation decisions, the limitations and what comes next
You will defend

Why this architecture, why these services, why this model, why this security approach and why this cost structure.

Got questions?

Is this a beginner course?
No. It assumes at least a year of hands-on cloud computing experience and that you are comfortable in Linux and on the command line. If you are starting out, the Academy's Cloud Computing pathway is the right first step.
Do I need Terraform, Kubernetes or CI/CD experience?
No. Every hands-on exercise runs through the AWS Console, the CLI and managed services, and anything like deployment automation is taught from first principles in the week that needs it. n8n is used as a no-code tool, connected by API key and webhook.
Is it an AI course?
It is a cloud engineering programme about AI. You will not train models. You will use AI in your daily cloud work, build applications on Bedrock, connect them to your organisation's knowledge, give them controlled tools, secure them, and run them in production, which is the part a cloud engineer is actually asked to own.
Does it prepare me for an AWS certification?
Yes. The programme covers every domain of the AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) exam, with the exam-depth material in the recorded segments each week so the live time stays practical. You book and sit the exam with AWS separately.
What does a Saturday look like?
Live on Google Meet from 9am to 3pm UK time: a one-hour lecture, a two-hour lab, a second one-hour lecture and a second two-hour lab. Between Saturdays you have two hours of recorded material and an assignment lab. The final Saturday is the capstone presentations.
What do I need to have ready?
A reliable connection and a computer you can run a terminal on. The AWS sandbox and your n8n instance are provisioned for you, so you do not need your employer's accounts or your own card on a cloud provider.
What if I miss a Saturday?
Every session is recorded and available within 24 hours, and recordings stay open for 90 days after the programme. The assignment lab and the deliverable still apply, and the Slack community is where you catch up.
How does payment work?
Enrol online by card in £, $, € or ₦: £799 in full, or £430 now and £430 before Week 3. For an invoice or a bank transfer, send the form above and we reply with the details. Your place is confirmed when the first payment is received.
Can my employer pay, or send a team?
Yes. Two or more people from the same organisation get 10% off per head, and we can invoice the company directly. Choose "Corporate invoice" on the form.
Do I get a certificate?
On completion you receive the Cloudboosta AI-Native Cloud Engineer Certificate and a LinkedIn endorsement from Cloudboosta, after presenting and defending your capstone in the final session.
